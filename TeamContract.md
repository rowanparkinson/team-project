## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will use Discord as our primary communication channel.

* Team members will respond to messages within 1 day on weekdays.

* We will practice respectful and inclusive behaviour by listening to all team members' ideas, giving constructive and thoughtful feedback, and supporting each other when others ask for help. 

### Decision Making

* We will make decisions through consensus when possible, otherwise, majority vote. 

### Participation
* All team members will contribute ideas and engage in discussion during activities
* Members must notify others in advance if we won't be able to attend lecture or tutorial


---
## Conflict resolution

* We will resolve team conflicts by listening to each side of the argument and thoughtfully coming to a compromise through open discussion.
* If the conflict persists, we will reach out to a TA for support and advice. 

---

## Accountability
The responsibilities of each team member include:
* Complete assigned tasks on time and to a standardized quality
* Independently seek assistance from other group members or TAs/instructors when unsure of how to proceed
* Review other team members' Pull Requests when asked
* Providing honest and fair feedback and peer evaluations
